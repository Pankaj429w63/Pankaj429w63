🏗️ How I Think About Production AI
I don't want to stop at:
model.fit(X, y)
The engineering question is:
Can the model be reproduced?
        ↓
Can it be evaluated honestly?
        ↓
Can it be served behind an API?
        ↓
Can another service consume it?
        ↓
Can it be containerized?
        ↓
Can deployment be automated?
        ↓
Can failures be observed?
        ↓
Can secrets and dependencies be secured?
        ↓
Can the system scale?
        ↓
Can the whole thing be maintained?
That is the direction of my AI/ML engineering work.
